Inf-Dir shared viewer runtimes
==============================

This package (plugins\dist\inf-dir.runtime\) carries the external tools that more
than one viewer uses. It has no plugin.json and is not a plugin itself.

DjVuLibre 3.5.29  (djvulibre\)
------------------------------
Used by ebook-view to convert DjVu pages to PDF (ddjvu.exe).
DjVuLibre is distributed under the GNU General Public License v2 or later.
The isolated runtime contains its COPYING file.
Source: https://djvu.sourceforge.net/

GhostXPS 10.08.0  (gxps\)
-------------------------
GhostXPS is Artifex' interpreter for Microsoft XPS and OpenXPS. pdfjs-view runs
`gxpswin64.exe -sDEVICE=pdfwrite -o <out>.pdf <in>` to turn XPS/OpenXPS into PDF.
GhostXPS is Copyright Artifex Software, Inc. and is distributed under the GNU
General Public License v3 (or a commercial license; see COPYING and LICENSE in
the runtime directory). Source and binaries:
https://ghostscript.com/  https://github.com/ArtifexSoftware/ghostpdl-downloads
